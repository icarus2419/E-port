Modular Multiplayer Poker Platform

A Python/FastAPI poker platform that separates the table engine, variants, human/AI players, lobby, WebSocket updates, and variance-reduction demos.

What it shows

  • No-Limit Texas Hold’em plus a toy High Card variant
  • Human and AI player types with legal action generation
  • Room/lobby flow with browser UI and viewer-specific state
  • Seeded comparison demo using common random numbers and seat averaging
