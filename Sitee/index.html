About Me

I like turning messy ideas into working software.

I am Joseph Doyle-Samadi, also known online as Joseph Samadi, a London-based software engineer with a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Liverpool. My experience includes freelance web development and a software engineering role at Heaven Estate Agent, working across frontend interfaces, backend APIs and application deployment.

My projects include Knockround, a jobs and payments app for UK tradespeople on Cloudflare Workers, NoSite Leads, a Next.js and TypeScript SaaS, a Python multiplayer poker platform with Redis, SecureDoc, a Node.js document approval app, and SkillHanger, an open-source Swift macOS app. Each case study explains the problem, implementation and engineering decisions.
