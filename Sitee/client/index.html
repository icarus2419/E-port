Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

How much does it cost?

It's a single one-off payment, and I'll tell you the exact price up front, before you decide. There are no monthly or yearly fees from me.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. I build it before you've paid anything. If you don't want it, I take it down and you owe nothing.

Why did you make me a site without asking?

I look for good local businesses that don't have a website yet. Showing you a working preview is more useful than describing one, and you can decide in two minutes on your phone.

Where did my photos and details come from?

From your public Facebook page and Google listing. If anything's wrong, or you'd rather use different photos, tell me and I'll swap them.

Can I change things before it goes live?

Of course. Tell me what's wrong or out of date and I'll fix it before you pay anything.

What if my prices or hours change later?

Message me and I'll update it. Small changes like that are free.

Will it get me on Google?

It's set up so Google can read your business name, services and location. Ranking takes time and nobody can honestly promise a position, but a proper website gives you a far better chance than a Facebook page alone.
